## Expanded men — men-variety-v1.png

Use-case: game-asset. Generate a production portrait atlas for an Italian family strategy game. Square canvas, EXACTLY 5 columns and EXACTLY 5 rows, 25 equal square cells filling the full image edge to edge. NO gutters, borders, separators, titles, labels, text or numbers. Every cell is a separate front-facing centered head-and-upper-shoulders portrait on matching warm beige lightly textured parchment. Keep complete hair silhouettes inside each square with 6 percent top margin, shoulders at bottom, eyes at consistent height. Hand-drawn historical Italian archive illustration: fine pencil, charcoal, ink and softly faded watercolor, restrained realism and naturally colored skin, subtle warm light, detailed at small scale. Each ROW is one unique person, same identity across FIVE ages left-to-right: 8-year-old child, 16-year-old adolescent, 28-year-old adult, 50-year-old mature adult, 75-year-old elder. Preserve facial structure across that row, naturally age their skin and hair; adult clothing follows the row's specified muted color, children wear simple period-appropriate shirts. Distinctive unrelated faces with distinct jaw, noses, eyes and brows between rows. Palette softly muted, never monochrome sepia, enough visible hair and shirt color variety to distinguish game characters. Anatomy must be natural, clean hair silhouettes, uninterrupted uniformly colored hair at each age, grey naturally with age. Skin of neck is natural skin color, clothing colored ONLY on fabric below collar. No recoloring spill, no double-colored hair, no distorted or melted hair, no modern fashion, no digital gloss. MEN atlas. Row 1: long narrow face, straw-blond straight hair with modest side part, dusty blue shirt. Row 2: broad square jaw, thick black close-cropped hair, olive green shirt. Row 3: freckled rounded face, auburn red short loose curls, terracotta shirt. Row 4: aquiline nose and angular cheekbones, chestnut brown swept-back wavy hair, ochre waistcoat over cream shirt. Row 5: broad nose and soft kind eyes, dark brown longer ear-length straight hair middle parted, muted burgundy shirt; naturally thinning on top at age 50 and 75. The first two columns must be convincingly children/adolescents without adult beards; optional adult facial hair only in rows 2 and 4, matching their hair. Five obviously different men, five age stages each.
