Gymnopédie was converted from Ogg FLAC to Ogg Vorbis for Godot playback. The other source files are unchanged. `Ambience/courtyard.ogg` is a 60-second mix of the three credited ambience recordings: crossfaded breeze, additional birds, and a quieter filtered bell passage starting at 12 seconds, with fades at the boundaries. Rebuild it with `python3 Tools/BuildCourtyard.py` (requires ffmpeg). These field recordings evoke the setting; they were not recorded in Tuscany.

The Rêverie page leaves the recording's performer and provenance unspecified. Its listed public-domain status is recorded here without claiming independently verified performance rights.
