# Laws and family taxation

Open **Laws & obligations**, or **Read current laws** in Finances. The panel shows the current government, succession rules, actual household tax breakdown, the reform calendar and historical sources.

## Starting in 1800

The old flat one-lira levy has been replaced by three distinct pressures:

1. **Property/community assessment:** based on owned plots and buildings, using estimated rental capacity. Additional land, buildings and house rooms raise the basis; selling holdings reduces it. The family still owes property taxes when there are no residents. Property is assessed once in household accounts, not again as a business cost.
2. **Tuscan household macinato:** residents aged **three and over** count as taxable mouths. Eight paying classes reflect means; a separate exemption represents recognized destitution. The game estimates means from earnings, property rental capacity and savings. Cash debt alone is not an exemption. Births, third birthdays, departures, deaths and changes in circumstances affect the monthly equivalent of the assessment.
3. **Salt/consumption duties:** an estimated component of the household's food purchase bill, separately disclosed in Finances. It is charged once. The prototype does not reconstruct a municipal salt tariff or introduce a modern general sales tax.

The Tuscan macinato in 1800 is **not** the later Italian per-quantity milling tax. Municipal assessments were based on local quotas, annual rolls and certifications; the game's monthly recalculation is an approximation, not an exact historical collection procedure. Eligibility for recognized destitution is inferred rather than applied for through a separate game action.

## Calendar

| Date | Implemented change |
| --- | --- |
| January 1809 | The old Tuscan household macinato ends under French fiscal reorganization. Property contributions continue. French personal, openings and trade taxes are identified as context, not separately simulated. |
| September 1816 | Restored property rules are labeled prediale / dazio comunitativo, following the regulation of 16 September. Monthly precision applies the label in September. No unsupported reinstatement of the earlier household macinato is made. |
| March 1861 | The Kingdom of Italy begins. **No invented property-tax jump** occurs at proclamation. |
| April 1862 | Modeled 2% registration duty on cash estates after loans and reserved dowries, before distribution. Both the head's cash estate and relatives' personal estates pay it. |
| July 1864 | Modeled 6% ricchezza mobile duty on non-agricultural resident earnings and positive non-farm business profits. No tax on losses or gross sales. Farm earnings use the property assessment in this abstraction. |
| January 1866 | The unified civil code takes effect. The existing equal-share cash will already satisfies the modeled descendant protection; nomination changes leadership, not estate shares. |
| January 1869 | The Italian milling duty starts, based on estimated wheat consumed, independently of the old Tuscan means classes. |
| January 1884 | The milling duty ends. Only that duty disappears; underlying food prices are not reset. |
| July 1898 | Voluntary workers' old-age insurance is explained. No automatic pension payment, enrollment or contribution history is invented. |

Calendar-derived rules are reconstructed from saved dates. Crossing a milestone adds a Chronicle entry and notification and pauses the clock, independently of random events and player choices. Loading a later save does not replay earlier notices or rewrite ledger history. Completed months use their own tax rules: January's reform does not retroactively tax December.

## Rates and estimates

**Historical structure and dates are distinguished from estimated amounts.** The prototype's eight class thresholds and monthly per-mouth charges are not a transcription of an identified municipality's tax roll.

- Property basis: authored plot capital plus 180,000 accounting units per building level, and 20,000 per additional Landi House room beyond three. Capital is a proxy, not historical cadastral rent; condition and speculative selling prices do not immediately erase the assessment. Starting basis: 184,000.
- Estimated annual rental yield: 5% of that basis. Estimated property charge: 10% of rental capacity, divided into monthly equivalents and scaled by the price index. Initial charge: 77 accounting units, or 0.77 displayed game lire per month. These percentages are **balance estimates**, not a historical ten-percent universal levy.
- Means per taxable mouth, normalized to initial prices: wages + estimated monthly rent + 3% annual imputed income from positive savings. Below 800: recognized destitution (recorded as class 0). The next thresholds are 1,200 / 1,600 / 2,400 / 3,600 / 5,200 / 7,200 / 10,000, giving paying classes 8–2; above these, class 1. Monthly per-mouth charges are 15 / 20 / 25 / 35 / 45 / 60 / 80 / 100 units, scaled by the price index.
- Salt/consumption component: 2 units per adult-equivalent consumer, scaled by the price index. This amount and its continuity across eras are simplified estimates. Children consume less; children under three are outside the wheat-consumption estimate.
- Italian wheat tariff: the **initial statutory 2 Italian lire per 100 kg**, converted into the fixed accounting basis using 1 Tuscan lira = 0.84 Italian lire. Consumption is estimated at 10 kg per adult equivalent per month. Mixed cereals, mill-level enforcement, intermediate tariff reductions and persistent retail markups are not reconstructed; the initial tariff is retained until abolition as an explicitly disclosed simplification.

Direct assessments are not discounted by the purchase bonus or by economizing. Buying less food in debt does reduce estimated consumption duties through quantity, not through an artificial discount to the tax rate. Duties reduce the monthly surplus and education affordability. Household budgets used by career checks, event checks, UI and simulated players all include the owned-property basis. Business income duties appear separately in sales/payment transactions.

Estate rates, income rates, property yields, class thresholds and consumption estimates remain provisional. Wages have not been increased to offset taxes. Full historical estate assessment, property fragmentation, spouse usufruct, gift collation, municipal quotas, local exemptions, civil registration and marital authorization are not reconstructed. Later twentieth-century runs retain the prototype's rules rather than implementing every subsequent reform.

## Sources and verification

- [Tuscan macinato inventory](https://ast.sns.it/view/ca/8050): family composition and eight income classes, exclusion of children under three, and separately recognized destitution.
- [Montaione macinato records](https://archivitoscana.sns.it/view/ca/Montaione_CA_12_Tassadelmacinato_04): administration by communities from 1789 and assessments based on composition and income.
- [Tuscan property assessments](https://archivitoscana.sns.it/view/ca/3475): property assessments through 1808.
- [French fiscal reorganization, archival inventory](https://ricerca.sns.it/retrieve/d3eba5d6-3e32-4b30-9891-b3a642588c06/Strisciata%20Inventario.pdf): the decree of 22 August 1808 replaces earlier contributions from 1 January 1809.
- [Ministry of Culture prediale inventory](https://inventari-san.cultura.gov.it/inventari/296/ca/1085158): the regulation of 16 September 1816, prediale and dazio comunitativo.
- [University of Florence: milling tax](https://www.sba.unifi.it/index.php?func=viewpage&module=CMpro&pageid=577): 1869 commencement, initial wheat tariff, intermediate reductions and 1884 abolition.
- [Italian inheritance tax research appendix](https://www.ecineq.org/wp-content/uploads/papers_EcineqLSE/EcineqLSE-332.pdf): registration law no. 585 of 21 April 1862.
- [Ministry of Culture income-tax inventory](https://inventari-san.cultura.gov.it/inventari/296/ca/1085164): ricchezza mobile law no. 1830 of 14 July 1864.
- [1865 civil code, inheritance title](https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Codice_civile_(1865)/Libro_III/Titolo_II) and [commencement decree](https://www.normattiva.it/atto/caricaDettaglioAtto?atto.codiceRedazionale=065U2358&atto.dataPubblicazioneGazzetta=1865-07-01&tipoDettaglio=originario): representation, descendant equality and children's half-estate reserve, effective January 1866. See also `Succession-and-Loans.md` for earlier Tuscan succession limits.
- [INPS institutional history](https://www.inps.it/it/it/l-istituto/la-storia-e-i-numeri-dell-inps.html): voluntary insurance in 1898, rather than a universal pension in 1861.
