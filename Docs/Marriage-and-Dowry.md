# Marriage and dote — prototype model

Marriage joins two people and opens a connection between families. Its value depends on future choices and relationships, rather than an automatic occupation bonus.

| Relative marrying | Couple stays in Landi House | Couple settles nearby |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daughter / bride role | Shared purse pays dowry into the couple's separate capital; one extra resident needs space | Shared purse pays dowry; daughter leaves household; couple holds capital |
| Son / groom role | Bride's family pays into shared purse; the exact sum is owed to the couple; one extra resident needs space | Bride's family pays couple's separate capital; no payment enters player's purse; son leaves household |

An already departed relative can marry and return with a spouse, requiring two free places. The head cannot settle elsewhere. All cases retain names, parent links, branch membership and spouse links. Household membership determines whose wages and food enter the shared accounts.

Dowry is funded before marriage. Negotiation changes public terms; personal acceptance is separately determined from private motives. Repeated requests use the existing person/month limit. The current model distinguishes willing acceptance, reluctant compliance and refusal, and records responses in memory and Chronicle. Compatibility is an internal comparison of values. Current studies, romance, independence, tradition, resentment, trust and an expressed preferred spouse can affect willingness. Personality-dependent secrecy and a richer courtship conversation remain future work.

The incoming sum can finance the house while the couple resides there, but the family owes the full principal back. Moving out releases it, even into a negative purse balance. Returning reverses that transfer once. Death releases a resident incoming obligation once into a reserve for the survivor or descendants. This reserve is represented by couple capital; detailed estate distribution is not simulated. An outgoing daughter's capital stays separate, including if her couple resides at home.

Village households retain their independent cash and relationship data. Their occupation and standing profiles define starter marriage opportunities. Successful requests for allied help build family standing; Conti requires standing before discussing marriage. A union adds a family connection, while monthly couple experience changes its strength. Death does not erase the kinship connection.

Favors use the existing family's trust and resentment, available cash and alliance strength. Refusal consumes the annual request slot. A granted favor can require repayment; it blocks subsequent favors until repaid, and delayed repayment raises resentment. Agricultural/trade earnings debit the allied household and credit the player's purse by the same amount. Reparations and tutoring develop skills; repairs change actual house condition and village history. Tutoring opens the existing `institution` career access resource but does not bypass education or qualification requirements. Recommendations develop persuasion and standing. Full political institutions, bank credit, apprenticeships and land leases are future extensions.

Separated couples live in abstract nearby branch households. They can talk with the head and have children while the simulation progresses. Children inherit portraits through the existing parent system, remain in the same branch and live with their parents. No wages or ordinary food bills from these branches affect Landi House purse. Incoming couple capital is conserved across moves; a separate complete branch budget is not yet simulated.

The save record stores offers, unions, alliances and standing. Validation checks referenced people/families, spouse reciprocity, personal relationship data, capital state and obligation ranges before restore. Village candidate lives are generated per proposal, becoming named people only upon marriage. All monetary figures are balance settings.
