# Art Direction — The Landi family

## North star

The game is about memory, family, warmth, conflict, legacy, and time. It should feel **warm, nostalgic, intimate, and slightly melancholic**.

The intended style is an illustrated European domestic realism: a storybook strategy game for adults. It draws from late nineteenth and early twentieth century Italian homes, faded photographs, plaster walls, wood furniture, paper documents, and sepia memories. It is neither full realism nor cartoon.

## Visual identity

- Warm earth tones and slightly desaturated colour.
- Painterly, illustrated portraits.
- Textured paper UI with wood, plaster, and fabric character.
- Gentle aging, patina, faded ink, and soft borders.
- No neon, glossy modern surfaces, or sterile product-interface styling.

## Palette

### Base neutrals

These should dominate the UI: warm cream, aged paper, soft beige, dusty taupe, walnut brown, and charcoal brown instead of pure black.

### Emotional colours

Use terracotta, olive green, dusty blue, burgundy or wine red, muted gold, and faded teal. Keep them desaturated and deliberate.

### Meaningful accents

| Meaning | Colour family |
| --- | --- |
| Conflict, debt, crisis | Deep wine red |
| Legacy, heir, prestige | Muted gold |
| Harmony, stability, good outcomes | Soft olive green |
| Distance, coldness, estrangement | Blue-grey |
| Memory, grief, old history | Violet or plum |

## Time moves visually

The game begins with early twentieth century materials: old Italian homes, plaster walls, dark furniture, sepia portraits, handwritten documents, muted clothing, and oil lamps or early electricity. Through the century, interiors become lighter and more modern; furniture, radios, televisions, appliances, photos, and materials gradually change. Old heirlooms remain alongside newer objects.

The palette should age with the family. This visual evolution is part of the game’s theme.

## Italian domestic atmosphere

Aim for an authentic old Italian family home: warm plaster, ceramic tiles, dark wood cabinets, lace curtains where appropriate, framed portraits, a family dining table, shutters, balconies, and courtyards shaped by region and class.

Avoid postcard clichés: no flag colours everywhere, tourism-brochure Tuscany, Vespas as decoration, or exaggerated village nostalgia.

## UI language

The UI is a family archive, household ledger, old photo album, and inheritance document.

- Paper and notebook texture.
- Subtle ink lines, stamped labels, soft borders, and restrained ornament.
- Framed portraits and folder or accordion-like layouts.
- Handwritten accents only for family notes and chronicle entries.
- Decorative elements support clarity; the interface should not become a museum display.

## Portraits

Use semi-stylized painted portraits: not pixel art, 3D, anime, or hyper-realistic photography. They must communicate age, resemblance, class, era, mood, health, and inherited family traits across generations.

The aim is a family wall whose members visibly belong together across time.

## Avoid

- Too much grey or undifferentiated brown.
- Super-saturated colours.
- Flat minimalist SaaS-like UI.
- Grimdark treatment.
- Cozy-cute treatment.
- Hyper-realistic art that is expensive and emotionally stiff.

## One-line direction

**A warm, painterly, slightly desaturated Italian domestic-historical style, beginning in early 1900s cream, wood, terracotta, olive, and burgundy, then gradually modernising across generations.**
